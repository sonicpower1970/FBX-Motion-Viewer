The MP4 tests inspect actual H.264 output packets, not a mocked encoder:

- 1280×720, 30fps, 2-second synthetic take: 61 frames, 2.033333 seconds, no audio. Independently decoded a middle frame with the locally installed FFmpeg for image inspection. FFmpeg is **not** part of the application or distribution.
- 1920×1080, 24000/1001fps, 1-second take: 25 frames, timestamps match frame index/rate.
- Current Viewport, local production FBX 6000 skeleton: 933 frames, 31.1-second output, Follow enabled. Viewer returned to frame 300 afterward.
- Export camera movement is checked numerically. Original animation time, Play state, camera position/quaternion/zoom, Orbit target, Follow reference/mode, controls and viewport size match exactly after success, cancellation and simulated WebCodecs unavailability.
- Bones OFF hides bones even with X-Ray ON. Actual shadow on/off image difference is tested, including single-sided geometry.
- Offline ZIP tests block nonlocal requests and exercise modern FBX, legacy skin/takes, movie generation/download and the supplied production skeleton.

These results do not certify all FBX exporters, large-file performance, every codec/GPU/OS combination, or every rig's automatic Follow target. See [behavior, limits and manual acceptance](v0.2.md).
